LET’S BREAK DOWN OUR STORY

At Gorgeous Growth, the name says it all, and it’s intentional. “Gorgeous” is about more than appearances; it’s our standard for excellence, &, and aligned connections. “Growth” speaks to our mission: helping ethical go-getters, brands, and businesses scale intentionally, with clarity and confidence. We’re here to help you combine strategy, community, & the right support, because growth doesn’t just happen… it’s grown gorgeously.

Where we Stem from

Historically, the term ‘Gorgeous’ began as our Founder’s mantra and expectation of executional excellence. She relied on this terminology to set the tone and define a balanced mindset for every relationship or experience, whether digitally or in-person. Upon graduation from the Business School of DePaul University and completion of organizational presidency, our founder quickly uncovered the discourse and gap between ethical business and community. Community-centric, sustainable professional growth was not accessible to the very leaders and builders who shape Tomorrow’s professional landscape.

Gorgeous Growth is a natural evolution of this foundational discovery. Implementing core areas of expertise in intentional leadership, business strategy, and community management, we set out to help scale professional growth with clarity under ‘The Gorgie Method’. The method’s mapping, ‘Set, Scale, Match’, is designed to provide accessible, sustainable growth from clarity on first steps to campaign execution and matchmaking.

Where we’ve blossomed to today

Rooted in foundational elements of excellence in synergy with the value of community and transparent business practices, Gorgeous Growth Group has blossomed into an entire ecosystem designed for professional growth. Today we remain committed to making a key difference in the professional landscape, and with the notion of ethical business and commerce practices to foster an uplifting, successful community of growers and changemakers.

Supported by our three branches of growth—Knowledge, Network, and Toolkits—our team refers to our naming as a principle of exceptional growth, attention to detail, and a reminder of remaining true to your core values and putting community over competition. At Gorgeous Growth Group, we believe excellence, alignment, and connection remain at the center of every relationship, project, and endeavor we grow.

Master Terms of Services

At Gorgeous Growth, we are committed to building a thoughtful, strategic, and community-driven ecosystem where founders, creatives, and professionals can connect, collaborate, and grow—gorgeously. These Terms of Service outline the structure, expectations, and responsibilities that support our consulting and matchmaking services. They are designed to protect both our community and our business, ensuring that every interaction within Gorgeous Growth is aligned, transparent, and mutually beneficial. By engaging with our services, joining our network, or entering into a consulting or matchmaking agreement, you acknowledge and agree to the terms outlined below. We encourage you to review them carefully and reach out to our team at hello@gorgeousgrowth.org with any questions.

1. Services and Offerings

Under these branches, Gorgeous Growth provides a hybrid ecosystem of services spanning the following distinct streams:

Consulting Services: Delivered by in-house consultants; our consulting tiers include Power Hour Mutual, Power Hour, Glam Plan, Signature Package, and Empire Edits. All consulting packages are scoped, priced in advance, and must be paid in full upon scheduling. Payments are non-refundable and non-transferable.

Matchmaking Services: Pairing Members with vetted talent, specialists, or brands based on strategic goals, facilitated through our curated intake process.

Growth Network: Exclusive professional network memberships fostering growth, collaboration, and resources. First-dibs access to networking and experiential events, including The Builder’s Dinner Series and the Second-Hand Soirée.

Digital Products: Access to the Gorgeous Growth ‘Growth Shop,’ featuring proprietary digital tools and templates.

2. Membership Fees & Payment

Members engaging Gorgeous Growth for matchmaking or networking services agree to pay a non-refundable annual or monthly membership fee. This fee includes inclusion in the Gorgeous Growth network, access to community resources, and priority consideration for matches. Delivery of service is defined by the provision of matches, completion of consulting sessions, delivery of strategic materials, or execution of events.

3. Matchmaking Earnings & Commission Protection

Members utilizing our matchmaking services agree to remit a minimum 5% commission on the total gross earnings of all paid engagements resulting directly from introductions made by Gorgeous Growth. This obligation applies to the entire duration of the engagement resulting from the introduction, encompassing cash, trade, or alternative compensation. Participants must report all earnings generated from Gorgeous Growth connections within 10 business days of payment. Gorgeous Growth reserves the right to utilize check-ins, booking confirmations, or communication logs to enforce commission claims. Commission payments not received within 14 days of the invoice date will incur a $25 monthly late fee. Continued non-payment may result in immediate termination from the network and legal action to recover funds, including attorney’s fees.

4. Referral Tracking

We believe one of the most beautiful parts of Gorgeous Growth is building a community of driven professionals ready to flourish together. Members who refer new clients may be eligible for a referral bonus (a gifted consulting session or non-taxable appreciation gift), issued after the referred party signs a formal agreement and completes payment. Referrals must be reported through our official platform for tracking.
